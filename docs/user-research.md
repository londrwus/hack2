# User research: street interviews (on a construction site)

## Video 1 (80s): logistics on site
- Manages logistics: materials, glass, large machinery; when it arrives and how it moves around the site safely.
- Asked about the most painful/time-consuming part: "The construction industry is a high-risk industry, so your priority should be safety… I can't say anything we do here is not worth the time."

## Video 2 (32s): staying alert
- "You need to be in the right state of mind… You have to be aware of your environment all the time."
- Lapses in alertness are "a big issue… in every sphere of life."

## Video 3 (14s): working at height
- Work at height ("50 plus"). The audio is partly unclear: "hammers" may mean harnesses.
- "You don't want to put somebody there that is not the right step": the right person has to be chosen for high-risk tasks.

## Caveats
- Small sample: three short clips, mostly from one person.
- No specific tool or pain point was named.
